THE VANISHED SCHOOL MASCOT

Shadow School Halloween Investigation

Student Detective Workbook · A2 · Listening · Grammar · Speaking

Your Mission
It is Halloween evening at Shadow School. The mascot, Shadow the Owl, has vanished from the Great Hall. The parade starts at 7:30. Listen carefully, collect clues, and find the owl. Do not guess — use only what you hear.

Task 2. Listen & Order — “The Evening Sequence”

Skill: Listening · Stage: Input
🎧 Listen again
Number the events 1–6 in the order you hear them.

A. Alex arrived and looked for his gloves.

B. Cora saw a shadow past the window.

C. Mrs Grey last saw the owl.

D. The lights flickered.

E. Ben finished cleaning and left.

F. There was a loud noise near the hall door.

Hint: The first event is the earliest time.
